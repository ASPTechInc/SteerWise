# v1.0.0

First release of SteerWise, a free and open-source (FOSS) driving practice app for learner drivers built with Flutter.

### Features

- Animated splash screen (a night road drawn in the app; tap to skip).
- Bottom navigation between the Home, Show Me/Tell Me and Settings screens.
- Voice-guided instructions: spoken prompts such as "Turn left at the junction" and "Take the second
  exit at the roundabout", stepped through manually or automatically every 8 seconds in Auto Mode.
- Live front-camera preview on the Home screen to watch your steering technique while you
  practise, with a shortcut to the device settings if camera access is turned off. The camera
  runs at a low resolution with audio off and turns off when you switch tab or leave the app. A
  button switches between the front and rear cameras, and the choice is remembered.
- The Home screen's driving simulation pauses while another tab is showing.
- Driving simulation: a UK left-hand-traffic road with oncoming cars that stop at red and amber
  traffic lights, pedestrians, a score, collision detection with Retry, a zoomable mini map and
  lane-to-lane steering by button or swipe.
- Get Feedback button with a basic steering tip.
- If the device has no text-to-speech engine, SteerWise explains how to install a free one (such as
  RHVoice or eSpeak NG from F-Droid).
- Show Me/Tell Me questions from the UK practical driving test, with search, a Show/Tell filter,
  a practice mode that reveals answers, and read-aloud questions in a British English voice.
- Light, dark and system themes, with your choice saved on the device.
- Side menu with Help, Licence, Privacy policy, About (version, what's new, open-source licences
  and source code) and Feedback (opens the GitHub issue form).
- No accounts, analytics or adverts. Released under the MIT licence. The Show Me/Tell Me questions
  contain public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
