# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25-10-2026

SteerWise is a free and open-source (FOSS) driving practice app built with Flutter.
This policy explains what data the application handles and where it goes.

## Summary

- SteerWise has no accounts, analytics, adverts or trackers.
- Your settings stay on your device.
- The camera preview is shown on screen only. SteerWise does not record, save or upload images or
  video, and does not use the microphone.
- SteerWise does not send your data to its developers or to any server.

## Data stored on your device

SteerWise stores the following using `shared_preferences`:

- Your theme choice (light, dark or system)
- Whether to use the front or rear camera for the preview

Nothing else is saved by the app. Driving simulation scores are kept in memory and are cleared when
you leave the screen or close the app.

## Camera

The Home screen shows a live preview from the front camera (or the rear camera, if you switch to it)
so you can watch your steering technique while you practise. The preview is processed on the device and is not recorded, stored or sent
anywhere. The camera is turned off when you switch to another tab or leave the app.

Your choice of front or rear camera is saved on your device with your other settings. SteerWise
does not use the microphone.

## Voice instructions

Spoken instructions and Show Me/Tell Me read-outs use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine.
SteerWise passes the instruction text to that engine and nothing else. Some text-to-speech engines
can process text online, depending on the engine and the settings you have chosen on your device.
This is covered by the engine provider's privacy policy.

## Network connections

SteerWise does not make network connections of its own. The Show Me/Tell Me questions, the
driving simulation, the help pages and the licence and privacy documents are bundled with the app
and work offline.

The Feedback and Source code links open GitHub in your browser. What happens there is covered by
GitHub's privacy policy.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** used to show the live camera preview described above. You can refuse or revoke this
  permission at any time. The rest of the app keeps working without it.

## Open source

You can read the source code to check how SteerWise handles data:
https://github.com/ASPTechInc/SteerWise

## Changes to this policy

This policy is updated when the app's data handling changes, for example if map views or session
recording from the [planned features](docs/md/planned-features.md) are added. The latest version is
in the repository.
